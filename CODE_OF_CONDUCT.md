# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

The rbAmp community welcomes contributions and discussions from anyone interested in open AC energy monitoring. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Standards

**Encouraged:**
- Technical, constructive feedback
- Patience with newcomers (someone always knows less than you, and that's OK)
- Acknowledgment of others' work
- Respectful disagreement based on evidence

**Not acceptable:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or content
- Trolling, baiting, or deliberate disruption
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- Spamming, advertising unrelated products
- Off-topic political discussions

## Scope

This Code applies to:
- All rb-amp GitHub repos (Issues, PRs, Discussions)
- Any space where you represent the rbAmp community

## Enforcement

Reports: **info@rbamp.com**

We will:
1. Review reports within 72 hours
2. Take action proportional to violation: warning → temporary ban → permanent ban
3. Not retaliate against good-faith reporters
